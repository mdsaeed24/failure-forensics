# Road Transport Services Agreement

This Road Transport Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 15 September 2026 by and between Deccan Freight Carriers ("Carrier") and Sunrise Agro Exports ("Customer").

## 1. Services
Carrier shall provide road transport services for Customer's containerized cargo from Customer's facilities to designated ports or other destinations as mutually agreed. Services shall be performed in a professional and timely manner.

## 2. Term
The term of this Agreement shall commence on 15 September 2026 and shall continue until 14 September 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

## 3. Rates and Payment
Customer shall pay Carrier a rate of ₹18,500.00 per container transported. Invoices shall be issued upon completion of each shipment, and payment is due within 30 days of invoice date.

## 4. Carrier Responsibilities
Carrier shall provide suitable vehicles, ensure proper handling and securing of cargo, and comply with all applicable laws and regulations.

## 5. Customer Responsibilities
Customer shall provide accurate shipping instructions, ensure cargo is properly packaged and ready for transport, and pay all amounts due under this Agreement.

## 6. Liability
Carrier shall be liable for loss or damage to cargo occurring during transport, subject to standard limitations of liability for road carriers. Customer shall indemnify Carrier for claims arising from improper packaging or misdeclaration.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon 30 days written notice for material breach that remains uncured after 15 days.
