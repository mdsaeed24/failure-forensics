MEMORANDUM

To: All Staff
From: Sandra Okonkwo, HR Director
Date: 2 March 2026
Subject: New Remote-Work Policy Effective 1 April 2026

I am pleased to announce that Granite Peak Insurance will implement a new remote-work policy, effective 1 April 2026. This policy reflects our commitment to flexibility and employee well-being while maintaining our high standards of service.

Key points:
- Eligibility: All full-time employees in non-customer-facing roles may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval.
- Core hours: Employees must be available during core hours (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time) on remote days.
- Equipment: The company will provide standard remote-work equipment upon request. No additional stipend will be issued.
- Workspace: Employees are responsible for ensuring a safe and ergonomic workspace.
- Performance: Existing performance metrics remain unchanged.

This policy does not alter compensation, benefits, or employment status. Managers will provide department-specific guidelines. Please direct questions to your HR representative.

We believe this policy will enhance work-life balance and productivity. Thank you for your continued dedication to Granite Peak Insurance.

Sandra Okonkwo
HR Director
