## Independent Contractor Agreement

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**Client:** Northwind Studio LLC  
**Contractor:** Carlos Mendoza

### 1. Services
Contractor agrees to provide graphic design services as requested by Client, including but not limited to logo design, marketing collateral, and digital assets. Contractor will perform services with professional skill and care.

### 2. Term
The engagement begins on 1 March 2026 and ends on 31 August 2026, unless terminated earlier under Section 5.

### 3. Compensation
Client shall pay Contractor at an hourly rate of $85.00. The total compensation shall not exceed a monthly cap of $6,000.00. Contractor shall invoice Client monthly, and Client shall pay within 30 days of receipt.

### 4. Independent Contractor Status
Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Contractor is responsible for all taxes and benefits.

### 5. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice. Upon termination, Client shall pay Contractor for all work completed up to the termination date.

### 6. Intellectual Property
Upon full payment, all work product becomes the property of Client. Contractor retains a non-exclusive license for portfolio use.

### 7. Confidentiality
Contractor shall not disclose Client's confidential information.

### 8. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties.
