## Milk Supply Agreement

**This Milk Supply Agreement** ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of 1 April 2026, by and between **Valewood Dairy** ("Supplier") and **Ridgeway Foods Ltd** ("Buyer").

### 1. Term
The term of this Agreement shall commence on 1 April 2026 and continue until 31 March 2028, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

### 2. Supply of Milk
The Supplier agrees to sell and deliver to the Buyer, and the Buyer agrees to purchase from the Supplier, milk crates in such quantities as the Buyer may order from time to time. The Supplier shall use its best efforts to meet the Buyer's requirements, subject to the terms herein.

### 3. Price
The price per crate of milk shall be £18.50. Prices are exclusive of any applicable taxes or delivery charges, which shall be the Buyer's responsibility as provided in this Agreement.

### 4. Minimum Annual Purchase
The Buyer guarantees a minimum annual purchase of £50,000.00 worth of milk crates during each twelve-month period of the term. If the Buyer fails to meet the minimum annual purchase, the Buyer shall pay the difference to the Supplier within thirty days after the end of the relevant period.

### 5. Payment Terms
The Buyer shall pay for each delivery within thirty days of receipt of the Supplier's invoice.

### 6. Binding Effect
This Agreement sets forth the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements. This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.
