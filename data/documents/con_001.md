# Cleaning Services Agreement

This Cleaning Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**Van Dijk Logistiek B.V.**, with its principal place of business at Distelweg 78, Amsterdam ("Client"), and

**Helder Facility Services**, with its principal place of business at Nijverheidsstraat 12, Rotterdam ("Contractor").

## 1. Term
This Agreement shall commence on 1 September 2026 and shall continue until 31 August 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 4.

## 2. Services
Contractor shall provide cleaning services for Client's logistics facility located at Distelweg 78, Amsterdam, including but not limited to sweeping, mopping, waste removal, and sanitation of common areas, three times per week.

## 3. Compensation
Client shall pay Contractor a monthly fee of €3,200.00. Payment shall be due within 30 days of receipt of invoice.

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice. If Client terminates without cause, Client shall pay Contractor an early termination fee of €500.00.
