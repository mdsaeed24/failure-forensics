# Commercial Lease Agreement

This Commercial Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is made and entered into as of 1 July 2026, by and between **Harbourside Property Trust** ("Landlord") and **Blue Gum Physiotherapy** ("Tenant").

## 1. Premises

The Landlord leases to the Tenant, and the Tenant leases from the Landlord, the premises described as Suite 4, 12 Harbourview Parade (the "Premises"), for use as a physiotherapy clinic.

## 2. Term

The term of this Lease commences on **1 July 2026** and terminates on **30 June 2029**, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Lease.

## 3. Rent

The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord monthly rent of **A$5,400.00**, payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month. Rent shall be paid by electronic funds transfer to an account designated by the Landlord.

## 4. Security Deposit

Upon execution of this Lease, the Tenant shall deposit with the Landlord **A$16,200.00** as security for the performance of the Tenant's obligations. The Landlord may apply the deposit to cure any default by the Tenant and shall return the unused balance within 30 days after the Lease term ends.

## 5. Use

The Premises shall be used solely as a physiotherapy clinic and for no other purpose without the Landlord's prior written consent.

## 6. Maintenance and Repairs

The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good order and repair, including all interior surfaces, fixtures, and equipment, at its own expense. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs and the exterior of the building.

## 7. Insurance

The Tenant shall obtain and maintain comprehensive general liability insurance naming the Landlord as an additional insured.

## 8. Default

If the Tenant fails to pay rent or any other sum due within 10 days after written notice, or breaches any other term of this Lease and fails to cure within 30 days after written notice, the Landlord may terminate this Lease and re-enter the Premises.

## 9. Assignment and Subletting

The Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 10. Governing Law

This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which the Premises are located.
